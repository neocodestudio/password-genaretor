Vault & Data

SecureGen ("the App", "we", "our") is a password generator and vault app for Android built with Flutter. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App interacts with, how it is handled, and what protections are in place.

SecureGen operates on a privacy-by-design principle. No backend server. No cloud service. No transmission of your vault data — ever. The App does display ads via Google AdMob, which is fully disclosed in Section 07.

✅
Core Promise

Your saved passwords, Master Password hash, and all vault data will never be collected, transmitted, sold, or stored on any external server. The only third-party data interaction is Google AdMob for ads.

Your vault data stays on your device. AdMob may collect device-level ad data independently (see Section 07).

SecureGen requires an active internet connection (WiFi or Mobile Data) for two purposes: (1) it's a product-level design requirement, and (2) Google AdMob uses it to load and display ads.

✅
Your Vault Data Is Never Transmitted

Even with internet required, SecureGen never sends, uploads, or syncs your vault data to any server. You can verify this with a network traffic inspector.

The connectivity_plus package is used only to detect if a connection is active. If no connection is found, core features are disabled until connectivity is restored.

Advertising

SecureGen displays ads via Google AdMob, operated by Google LLC. AdMob may independently collect data under Google's own Privacy Policy.

AdMob has zero access to your saved passwords, Master Password, or any vault content. It only interacts with Android OS-level advertising identifiers.

Legal & Rights

Every permission SecureGen requests is listed here with its exact purpose:

INTERNET
Detect active connection and load AdMob ads. No vault data is transmitted.
NETWORK_STATE
Check if WiFi or Mobile Data is active before enabling core features.
AD_ID
Required by AdMob on Android 13+ to access the Advertising ID. You can reset or opt out in Settings → Privacy → Ads.
USE_BIOMETRIC
Optional fingerprint / Face ID unlock. Only used when enabled in Settings.
USE_FINGERPRINT
Legacy biometric support for Android 6.0–8.x devices. Same purpose as USE_BIOMETRIC.
🖐️
Biometric Data

The App never accesses or stores biometric data. Fingerprint and face recognition are processed entirely by the Android OS. SecureGen receives only a pass/fail result.

SecureGen is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We have taken additional steps for AdMob compliance:

COPPA
AdMob is configured with child-directed treatment settings, disabling interest-based targeting for child users as required by the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act.
GDPR / TFUA
For EEA/UK users under digital consent age, we have enabled "tag for users under the age of consent in Europe" (TFUA) in AdMob, disabling personalised ads.

For concerns about ad data collected by Google for child users, review Google's family policies at families.google.com/familylink/privacy/child-policy.
